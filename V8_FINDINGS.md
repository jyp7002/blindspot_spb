# v8 findings — post-audit

**Distilled from `RESULTS_V8.md`** (the full lab record, which keeps every
retraction and engineering note in place). This file is the decision-oriented
version: what was established, what was not, and what the manuscript must change.

- **Experiments:** DEC, SUP1, ENV, SPC (committed) + INS (conditional), plus a
  registered robustness arm and a format-controlled replacement arm.
- **Run:** 2026-08-28/29, one L40S, 523 measured rows, 36 support dumps.
- **Preregistration:** `PREREGISTRATION.md` §v8, written before the first run —
  both DEC adjudication paragraphs, the hashed ENV split, and every amendment
  recorded as it was forced.
- **Audit:** adversarial refutation pass, 2026-08-29. **2 refuted, 3 weakened,
  2 confirmed.** All corrections are reflected below.

## 1. The headline: DEC returns Outcome B (DARE-divergent)

Behavioural edits **do not** behave like the task-performance deltas DARE studies.
A random 1% of the sign field recovers about a fifth of what the
magnitude-selected 1% recovers.

| condition | support | signs | mean removal |
|---|---|---|---:|
| **C-ref** | top-\|Δ\| | true | **+0.3220** |
| **C-a** | random | true, at those coords | **+0.0684** |
| C-tensorshuf ᵃ | top-\|Δ\| | pattern moved across projections | +0.0342 |
| C-layershuf | top-\|Δ\| | pattern moved across layers | +0.0043 |
| C-b | top-\|Δ\| | sign values permuted | +0.0036 |
| C-rand | random | random | +0.0014 |
| C-bottom | bottom-\|Δ\| | true | +0.0010 |

ᵃ n = 7 cells (phi's fused-QKV cells are structurally excluded — see §5).

**Δ_selection = +0.2537 [+0.1475, +0.3692]**, excludes 0, **unanimous 10/10 cells**
(48–103% of each cell's effect), across four benchmark families and both tiers.

### What the audit changed

The **sign is robust**; the **magnitude is conditional**. Report it that way.

| stress test | result |
|---|---|
| ε-corrected collateral gate (§6) | **+0.2835** [+0.1692, +0.4072] — *strengthens* |
| excluding phi (different module set) | +0.3175 — strengthens |
| non-occ axes only | +0.1228 [+0.0966, +0.1494] |
| excluding qwen-7B | +0.2319 |
| `natural` scale convention | Δ grows to +0.5031 / +0.2273 |
| aggressive α-continuation of all capped C-a runs | **+0.0957, covers 0** |

Two conditioning facts belong in the paper: the "79% of R(C-ref)" ratio is
computed over cells **pre-selected as in-envelope** (C-ref already positive), and
the magnitude is bounded by the frozen 4-α grid — C-a is purely *grid*-limited
(argmax at α=16 in 26/30 runs, consuming almost no collateral) while C-ref is
*collateral*-limited (in-budget at α=16 in only 20/30, ppl up to 1.358). That
asymmetry is a real limitation of the 4-α design.

### The honest qualifier that keeps this from overclaiming

**C-a − C-rand = +0.0670 [+0.0292, +0.1106], excludes 0.** The true sign field at
*random* coordinates still carries real signal — about a fifth of what it carries
at magnitude-selected ones. The field is **concentrated, not exclusive**. Say
concentrated.

Magnitude ranking is informative in both directions: C-ref (+0.322) ≫ C-a (+0.068)
> C-bottom (+0.001), with C-a − C-bottom also excluding 0. Nulls behave: C-rand
averages +0.0014 against a pre-registered expectation of ≈+0.008.

The effect lives in the **value and output** projections. Since this sub-selects

rests on a paired contrast rather than on a budget boundary (§6).

## 3. SUP1: the sign field tracks corpus content

36 support dumps. All overlap numbers are fold-enrichment over a hypergeometric
null (raw Jaccard is 0.015–0.079 and uninformative). Estimator validated by a
committed harness, `src/v8_selftest.py`, 45/45.

| comparison | fold (global) | fold (within-module) | signed agreement | n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| across seeds | 11.15× | 5.10× | 0.953 | 36 |
| across designers | 14.64× | 8.44× | 0.787 | 9 |
| across axes | 2.96× | 2.56× | **bimodal — see below** | 27 |

**The axis row is bimodal and the split is semantic:**

| axis pair | n | signed agreement |
|---|---:|---:|
| **bbq_Age × bbq_Race_ethnicity** | 3 | **0.769** |
| every other axis pair | 24 | 0.480–0.498 |

Two BBQ social-group axes share their sign field at designer-level strength over
~219k coordinates; unrelated axes sit at chance. **The sign field is not
axis-specific — it tracks corpus content.**

**Two caveats that limit this section.** The designer arm is 9 pairs from a
*single* cell (`qwen7b|occ_gender`) with per-pair spread 0.319–0.965 including a
sign reversal; the axis arm comes only from qwen-3B and phi-3B. **No target
appears in both arms**, so "designers agree more than axes" is inseparable from
"qwen-7B vs the 3B targets". Do not present it as a within-target dissociation.
`src/v8_sup1.py` also reports medians without bootstrap intervals.

**Structure:** support concentrates in **late layers** (early 0.41× → late 1.52×).
Projection density is `o_proj` 1.32× > `q_proj` 0.83× > `v_proj` 0.64× >
`k_proj` 0.49× — note `v_proj` has *fewer* surviving coordinates than `q_proj` yet
is causally load-bearing (§2). **Density is not importance**; a support heatmap is
not an importance map.

## 4. ENV: the gate does not survive held-out validation

v8 expected this to upgrade Contribution 4 from conditional to claimed. **It does
not.** Contribution 4 stays **conditional**.

- **Inventory: 36 cells** across four benchmark families (v8 estimated ~18–20).
  The widening comes from reading `contrast_gap` from the corpus each run actually
  consumed; the 114 frozen observations cross-check with **0 disagreements**.
- **On calibration the gate works exactly as theorised**: backfire 0.18 → **0.00**,
  utility +0.1462 → +0.1564, rejecting 4 of 17 cells (3 of them backfires).
- **On the frozen held-out split it is untestable** — no held-out cell falls below
  either threshold, so all policies degenerate to edit-all.
- **On the registered n=87 sensitivity it is negative**: the pre-skew gate buys
  **+0.0009** utility; the contrast-gap gate *costs* utility.
- **Not a bad draw.** Across 200 seeds of the split rule, a non-discriminating
  held-out half occurs only 6% of the time — but across the 187 splits that *do*
  discriminate, the gate's utility gain is **mean −0.0012**, exceeding +0.01 in
  6.4%. The gate is not hidden; it is not there.

`contrast_gap` remains a good **predictor** of removal (+0.507, n = 504). What
fails is turning it into an **abstention policy that beats editing everything**.
The manuscript should say the envelope is *described and calibrated* but not
validated out-of-sample. This is the eleventh operationalisation in this program
to fail its own pre-registered test, in the same way as the other ten:
convincing in-sample, absent out-of-sample.

## 5. Two protocol description errors

### 5a. The frozen protocol is not one module set

`resolve_targets(model, ATTN)` returns `['q_proj','k_proj','v_proj','o_proj']` for
eight of nine panel models but **`['qkv_proj']` alone for Phi-3.5-mini** — fused
QKV, with `o_proj` dropped entirely even though phi has one (`TARGET_FALLBACKS`,
`colab_t2t4.py:39`).

The manuscript's "attn (q,k,v,o) r16" is **false for phi**, which carries headline
numbers: C1 (+0.316 self / +0.632 cross_large), all four phi BBQ cells, `ss_intra`,
phi's §11 frontier rows, and phi as one of four C3/C5/C6 ablation targets.

**Action:** state the per-family resolved module set, or restrict "(q,k,v,o)" to
the eight families where it holds. This is a *description* error, not a superseded
figure, so it does not belong in the SUP quarantine table as that table stands.

### 5b. The base-only invariant never applied to the method panels

`FAM_MODELS` is instruction-tuned at every size. A base checkpoint appears in
exactly one place in the repo — `run_y1_bigbox.py`, for the science-line Y1 result.
And it is structural, not incidental: **`chat_prompt` raises without a chat
template**, and it feeds `mmlu_predict` and `elicit_selfdebias`, with MMLU inside
`fast_eval` — so **this pipeline cannot evaluate any base model**.

**Action:** strike any sentence claiming base-only measurement. Note that a future
base-model claim costs a new prompt path, not a new run.

## 6. A floating-point bug in the collateral gate — affects every panel

`collateral_ok` tests `(pre_mmlu - post_mmlu) <= 0.02`. MMLU is scored on **200
items**, so a 4-item drop *is* exactly the budget — but
`0.545 - 0.525 == 0.020000000000000018 > 0.02`. **Configurations sitting exactly on
budget are rejected.**

**238 probes across 25 panels**, including `v6trace/ablate` (36 probes), which
backs published `RESULTS_METHOD` numbers. Re-scored from the persisted α traces:

| result | as-run | ε-corrected |
|---|---|---|
| DEC Δ_selection | +0.2537 | **+0.2835** — strengthens |
| SPC ≤3.8B, 99% vs dense | −0.1286 (excludes 0) | **−0.0754, covers 0** |

Compounding: MMLU granularity (0.005 on 200 items) is coarse against a 0.02
budget, and the evaluation is **not fully deterministic** — two rows shared
between panels have identical `ppl_ratio` and `bias_reduction` but different
`dmmlu`, flipping the gate and swinging removal by 0.206.

**Recommended fix, deliberately not applied:** compare with an explicit tolerance
(`<= dmmlu + 1e-9`) or exact rational arithmetic, and raise the MMLU item count so
the budget is not a small multiple of the metric's granularity. Applying it moves
numbers program-wide — that is the author's decision, not a silent edit.

## 7. SPC: tier-dependent, and one qualifier withdrawn

**7–9B (n = 2 cells):** 99% sparsity is **free** — +0.0099 [+0.0063, +0.0135] —
at a **1.35 MB** patch. 99.9% costs −0.074. And **sparsity helps the budget**: all
7 budget-fail rows are llama at *low* sparsity (dense, 0.5, 0.9, 0.95, 0.97) and
**none at 99% or beyond**. The dense 8B edit is too disruptive to stay in budget;
sparsifying makes it deployable at all.

**≤3.8B: the claimed cost is withdrawn.** An earlier version reported −0.129
(excluding 0) and used it to qualify `RESULTS_METHOD` §5. That does not survive
§6's ε-fix (−0.0754, covering 0), n = 4 is below this project's own
`MIN_CELLS_ADJUDICATE = 8`, and the supporting "ablate re-slice reproduces it"
was **circular** — 46 of 48 shared rows are bit-identical, i.e. a deterministic
pipeline recomputing itself, not corroboration. **`RESULTS_METHOD` §5 stands as
written and needs no qualifier.**

## 8. INS: collateral clean, base comparison confounded

**INS-A — generation collateral (new).** The budget has always been
likelihood-only; IFEval had never been measured. Read the matched-norm contrast,
not the raw delta (`random_sign` also moves IFEval):

- **qwen7b_it: sign_only − random_sign = +0.0000** — no instruction-following cost,
  at +0.698 removal against the null's +0.004.
- **llama8b_it: −0.0220** — ≈1.1 SE, within noise.

Bounds: 2 targets, 1 axis, seed 0, 200 of 541 prompts (SE ≈ 0.020).

**INS-B — confounded; no registered reading is licensed.** Read naively the data
say instruction tuning pushes `bbq_Age` out of the envelope (base +0.385 vs
instruct +0.059). But **base MMLU is 0.190, below 4-way chance (0.250)** — the base
model cannot do the MCQ task in this format, so ΔMMLU can barely bind:

| target | ppl-bound failures | MMLU-bound | both |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| gemma2b_base | 18 | **3** | 0 |
| gemma2b_it | 7 | **26** | 17 |

The base arm faces a **ppl-only budget** and passes more often for that reason.
The `bbq_Age` gap sits exactly where instruct failures are MMLU-driven.

**What is licensed:** the base model *is* editable — +0.6047 against its own
matched-norm null of +0.0004 — so **the sparse-sign phenomenon is not an artifact
of instruction tuning**. This is the program's first base-model measurement on the
method line.

**What is not:** any statement about relative effect size across base and instruct,
so none of the three registered claim-ladder rungs. Fixing it needs a collateral
metric the base model can perform — a likelihood task, not chat-formatted MCQ.

*(A prompt-format control was necessary and revealing: format alone moves MMLU by
**−0.175** while leaving `pre_skew` identical. The naive fix would have charged
that to instruction tuning.)*
